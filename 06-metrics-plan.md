# Metrics Plan

## Step 1: What should change because it exists

The PM stops spending hours manually reading Slack threads and tickets each week, and leadership gets a cited, accurate feedback summary on time.

## Step 2: Goal metric

* **Metric:** PM hours spent producing the weekly feedback summary.
* **Without Juno:** about 3 hours a week (placeholder; time yourself for the next 2 weeks to get a real baseline).
* **With Juno:** under 45 minutes a week (review and edit only) within 4 weeks, with the digest still passing your rubric.

## Step 3: Usage metric and pattern to watch

* **Metric:** share of digests the PM forwards to leadership with 2 or fewer edits.
* **Pattern to watch first:** the PM re-checking sources by hand on every digest. If I’m still opening most of the cited threads, I don’t trust it yet, and the time savings aren’t real.

## Step 4: review date and stop line

* **Review date:** four weeks after the first live digest (for example, the Monday after the 4th run).
* **Stop:** if after 4 weeks the PM time hasn’t dropped below 1.5 hours a week, or any digest fails a deal-breaker (an invented figure, an uncited claim, or a miscounted theme that reached leadership), I stop using Juno for this job and go back to the manual process.
