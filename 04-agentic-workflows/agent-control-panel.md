## What Juno Is Doing

Juno turns a week of customer-facing Slack threads, support tickets, and call notes into a cited feedback digest for the PM, who decides what happens next.

## Four Levers

**Stop Conditions** (Loop):

Max 2 passes: draft, then a citation and ambiguity check. Max 40 tool calls. Abort a source if the same tool fails 2x in a row; name it as missing at the top of the digest. Hard timeout: 5 minutes. Fixed per-run cost cap; if hit, post a partial digest marked PARTIAL.

**Structured Tool Outputs** (Tools):

* list_slack_threads, list_support_tickets, list_crm_notes: IDs plus total_count. Zero results means “none found”; Or error with reason on failure
* read_slack_thread, read_support_ticket, read_jira_issue, read_crm_note: text plus source ID/URL on success; Or error with reason on failure.
* count_mentions: number plus the list of source IDs counted. Zero means no mention. Or error with reason on failure
* draft_digest: digest text plus its list of cited source IDs. Or error with reason on failure
* check_citations: {uncited_claims: [], count_mismatches: []}; both must be empty to pass. Or error with reason on failure.
* send_digest_to_pm: {status, delivered_to: pm_id}. Or error with reason on failure

**Confidence Thresholds** (Verification):

High: claim has a source and counts match, so it goes in. Medium (source unclear after pass 2): marked NEEDS CLARIFICATION and listed under Open questions. Themes with fewer than 3 mentions: marked LOW SIGNAL. Low (no source, or counts don’t match): excluded. If checks still fail after pass 2: posted to the PM only as “UNVERIFIED: N issues”.

**North Star** (Context):

Goal: give the PM a cited, accurate feedback digest they can forward to leadership with minimal edits.
Never allowed to: state a claim without a source, or invent ARR, contract terms, customer data, or PII.

## Rules of Engagement

**Agency Permission:**

Alone: read sources, count mentions, draft the digest, run the citation check. With PM approval: send the digest (to the PM only).
Blocked: any write to Jira, the CRM, or Slack, customer contact, and roadmap ranking.

**Access Control:**

Reads only customer-facing Slack threads, support tickets, Jira keys, and CRM call notes from a 7-day window.
Out of reach: private DMs, any system not in the verb list, and all write access.

**Fallback Protocols:**

Source unreachable: still run, name the missing source at the top, never guess its contents.
Cost cap hit: post a partial digest marked as such.
Checks fail after pass 2: post to the PM only, marked UNVERIFIED with the failing items.
Never post silently, never drop the output.

**Checkpoints:**

A person reviews before Juno continues when: any source is missing, any item is NEEDS CLARIFICATION, the citation check fails, or the digest would go to anyone other than the PM.
