## What graders score

- **What you’re scoring:** Juno’s weekly customer feedback digest
- **Value proposition:** Turn the week’s customer feedback into the top 3-5 themes, each with an accurate count and a source I can check, so I can forward it to leadership with minimal edits.

## Dimensions

| Dimension | Question | Scale | 1 or No | 3 | 5 or Yes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Source accuracy | Does every claim and quote come from a source that actually says it? | Yes / No | Any claim has no source, or the source doesn’t say it, or a quote is altered. | n/a | Each claim has a source ID, and opening that source confirms it. |
| Count accuracy | Do the mention counts match the distinct sources listed? | 1 to 5 | Counts are clearly wrong, or sources are missing or double-counted. | Counts are off by 1 on one theme, or sources are listed without a clear match. | Every count equals the number of distinct, listed sources. |
| Theme coverage and ranking | Are the biggest themes in the raw feedback present, and ranked sensibly by impact? | 1 to 5 | A major theme is missing, or a minor one is ranked first. | All major themes appear, but the ranking is debatable or one minor theme was included instead of a stronger one. | Top themes match what I’d find myself, ranked with a clear impact reason. |
| Boundaries respected | Does the digest avoid inventing data, ranking roadmap work, drafting customer messages, and counting internal asks or unconfirmed rep promises as customer demand? | Yes / No | Any invented figure, roadmap ranking, drafted reply, or internal ask counted as a customer mention. | n/a | No invented ARR, contract, or personal data. Next steps suggest a team to look at it without setting priority. The CEO email and unconfirmed promises are flagged, not counted. |

## Calibration

- **How many answers you’ll score, and how often:** The first 4 weeks, score every digest. After that, 3 digests a week, always including any marked UNVERIFIED or with NEEDS CLARIFICATION items.
- **Who scores them with you:** Me, plus one other PM or a Customer Success lead for the same digests once a week, scoring independently.
- **What happens when two of you disagree:** If scores differ on any question, we talk it through and rewrite that description so it’s clearer next time. If one of us flags a deal-breaker, it counts as a fail until we agree otherwise.

## Pass bar

An average of 4 or higher on questions 2, and 3, with no score of 1 on any scale question, and “Yes” on questions 1 and 4.

**Fails outright, whatever the average:**

- A single invented figure or quote, a claim with no matching source, any roadmap ranking or drafted customer reply, or an internal or unconfirmed request counted as customer demand.
