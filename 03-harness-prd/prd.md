## Problem & user

RocketShip PMs need this handled: Juno turns a week of Slack threads, tickets, and call notes into a cited feedback digest for the PM, who decides what happens next.. Juno does this inside limits a human set on purpose, and defends every call it makes.

## 01 Context · Data Requirements

**Required sources, and exclusions**

The week’s customer-facing Slack threads, support tickets, and CRM call notes. It does not see private DMs.

**How fresh must it be**

It reads a 7-day window at run time (Monday 8:00, or on demand). Changes after the run show up in the next run. If a source can’t be reached, Juno still runs, names the missing source at the top, and never guesses what it contained.

## 02 Tools · System Capabilities

**The verb list**

It can read, count, cite, and post a digest to the PM only. It can’t contact customers, edit Jira or the CRM, draft replies, or order roadmap work.
 * list_slack_threads
 * read_slack_thread
 * list_support_tickets
 * read_support_ticket
 * read_jira_issue
 * list_crm_notes
 * read_crm_note
 * count_mentions
 * draft_digest
 * check_citations
 * send_digest_to_pm

**Deliberate omissions**

No Jira or Slack write access, no customer-reply or announcement drafting, and no roadmap ranking. Juno recommends and the PM decides, and customer-facing mistakes (such as promising unconfirmed features) can’t be undone.

## 03 Loop · AI Costs & Latency

**Turn ceiling and escalation**

Max 2 passes: draft, then a citation and ambiguity check. Any source that is still unclear is marked NEEDS CLARIFICATION, listed under Open questions, and handed to the PM.

**Latency and cost target**

Digest ready within 5 minutes of the scheduled run. One scheduled run per week plus occasional on-demand runs, with a fixed per-run cost cap. If the cap is hit, it posts a partial digest marked as such.

## 04 Memory · Data Requirements

**What persists, at what scope**

Per PM only. It keeps the last 12 weeks of digests and the PM’s corrections. Nothing else carries over, and no PII is stored.

**Expiry and write rules**

Digests expire after 12 weeks, and corrections persist until the PM removes them. If a PM correction conflicts with Juno’s earlier output, the PM is stronger.

## 05 Permissions · AI Risks & Mitigations

**The permission tier**

| Side-effect class | Tier |
|---|---|
| read | auto |
| draft | auto |
| write | blocked |
| send | confirm |

**Justification**

Reading and drafting are internal and reversible, so they’re automatic. Write is blocked because Juno recommends and the PM decides. Send is confirm: the digest goes only to the PM, and any other recipient is not allowed. If a rep’s promise hasn’t been confirmed by engineering, Juno flags it to the PM and doesn’t count it as customer demand.

## 06 Verification · AI Testing & Measurement

**The check before output ships**

Before posting, it confirms every claim has a source, mention counts match the sources listed, themes under 3 mentions carry LOW SIGNAL, no ARR, contract, or PII data is invented, and the digest is under one page. It’s tested on a sample of 5 signals, and all checks must pass before it ships.

**Failure behaviour**

If a check fails, Juno uses its second pass to fix it. If it still fails, it posts to the PM only, marked “UNVERIFIED: N issues” with the failing items listed. It never posts silently and never drops the output.
